Parity Canvas

Privacy Policy for the Parity Canvas browser extension

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Parity Canvas is designed to do its work locally in your browser. It does not sell user data, does not use data for advertising, and does not transmit the pages you compare to any Parity Canvas server, because no such server exists.

What Parity Canvas does

Parity Canvas lets you load multiple webpages side by side in embedded viewports and mirrors your interactions — clicks, keyboard input, scrolling, and form actions — across those viewports, so you can compare how a page behaves across different screen sizes.

Information we access

To provide this functionality, Parity Canvas may access the following, only within your browser:

  • URLs you enter — the web addresses you type into the comparison panels, used only to load those pages into the extension's workspace.
  • Page content and DOM events — on pages you've added to a comparison set, the extension reads interaction events (clicks, scroll position, form input, keyboard events) in order to mirror them across the other panel(s).
  • Extension settings — your preferences such as mirror/scroll-sync toggles, viewport presets, and page scale, stored locally so they persist between sessions.

All of the above is processed and stored locally within your browser (for example, using browser storage APIs). None of it is transmitted to Parity Canvas developers or any third party, and none of it is used for advertising or sold to anyone.

Host permissions and scripting

Parity Canvas requests broad host permissions and uses content scripts because its core purpose — loading arbitrary pages side by side and mirroring interactions between them — requires interacting with whatever page you choose to load. These permissions are used exclusively to deliver that comparison and mirroring functionality, and are not used to monitor your general browsing activity outside the extension's workspace.

Data sharing

Parity Canvas does not share, sell, transfer, or rent any user data to third parties. There are no analytics, tracking, or advertising services embedded in the extension.

Data retention and deletion

Since all data is stored locally in your browser, it persists only as long as the extension remains installed and your browser storage is intact. You can clear it at any time by removing the extension or clearing its site data through your browser's extension settings.

Children's privacy

Parity Canvas is a developer/QA productivity tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If Parity Canvas's data practices change, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will reflect the change. Continued use of the extension after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
